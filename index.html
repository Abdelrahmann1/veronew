About GTR by Vero UK

A clearer route, built from lived experience.

GTR by Vero UK was created after strong interest from architects, designers, and creative professionals who wanted to understand the Global Talent Route more clearly — and prepare for it with structure rather than guesswork.

Why this programme exists

Talented people often underestimate their own evidence.

Many architects and designers have the achievements the route looks for — authored projects, leadership, recognition, innovation — but no clear way to organise them into a credible application.

This programme exists to make the journey structured, clear, and accessible: to help advanced creative professionals recognise their value and present it with confidence.

Team & community

A community of Global Talent holders.

GTR by Vero UK brings together a team of UK Global Talent endorsed professionals — architects and designers with an average of 15 years' international experience.

Founded by Mo Aby and shaped by endorsed members who have navigated the route themselves, the community continues to grow. As members go on to gain recognition in their own right, many choose to contribute their experience — broadening the perspective and expertise available to everyone we support.

The meaning of Vero

Our mission

To help exceptional creative professionals understand their value, structure their achievements, and build a clear pathway towards international career recognition.

Our vision

To become a trusted platform and community for globally ambitious architects, designers, and built-environment professionals seeking independent career pathways.
